How the mark reads

The letters U and P, drawn from a dusk on the ghats

  • The archA pointed Mughal arch, the gateway shape of Agra, Lucknow and Fatehpur Sikri, frames the whole mark.
  • The UThe Ganga's crescent bend at Varanasi, drawn in zari gold with the river flowing through it, cradling the setting sun.
  • The PThe river's right bank rises into a temple flagpole. Its saffron dhwaja curls round to form the bowl of the P.
  • Ghats and diyasThree steps and three floating diyas recall the evening aarti on the ghats, where the day in UP ends.
