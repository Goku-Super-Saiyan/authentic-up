How the mark reads

A lamp and a crane, spelling UP

  • The U is a diyaA clay lamp floating on the Ganga, as at the evening aarti in Varanasi. Its flame is a Banarasi buta, the paisley woven into every saree.
  • The P is a Sarus craneThe state bird of Uttar Pradesh. Its long neck is the stem of the P, its red-crowned head and sharp beak curl round to close the bowl, and it wades in the river.
  • The cusped archA scalloped Mughal arch like Lucknow's Rumi Darwaza, with a second pointed outline in thin gold, frames the night sky.
  • River and ghatsThe Ganga ripples under both letters and two ghat steps hold the scene, so the mark always reads as a place.
